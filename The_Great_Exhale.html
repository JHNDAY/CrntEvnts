Editor's Note The following verse has been fact-checked against the vibe in the room. Rhymes are final. Recounts will not be entertained.
I
Domestic Affairs

Trouble at Home

Although he'll complain, moan, and grouse, he won't be consoled by his spouse. We now know it's true that Trump lost her too, along with the Senate and House.

A clean sweep, as they say. All three chambers.

II
The Endorsement Effect

Kiss of Death, Delivered

November sent candidates packing because they had Donald Trump's backing. This past mid-term season daft Trump was the reason Republicans got a shellacking.

Sought after in the primaries; returned unopened in November.

III
Vox Populi

The Voters Have Spoken (With Gestures)

Due to promises constantly broken lots of anger at Trump had awoken. On November the third, when we flipped him the bird, the American voters had spoken.

Sign language is still language.

IV
Weather Desk

Rallying Against the Forecast

The month of October he spent at rallies he hoped would prevent a forecasted storm that later would form a Maga extinction event.

Paleontologists report the asteroid was, in fact, his own idea.

V
Ballot Notes

Write-In Wishful Thinking

His transgressions were too big to mend, yet he told voters "You should pretend that my name's on your ballot," but too few had a palate for the bullshit he spews with no end.

Voters pretended. Just not in the direction requested.

VI
Lone Star Upset

A Unique Talent

Old Trump isn't like me or you. He does things no other could do. For years people tried, and all were denied, till Donald Trump turned Texas blue.

Democrats have spent decades on this. Turns out they only needed him.

VII
Forecasting Desk

Nobody Saw It Coming

Not even the soothsaying swamis felt people that Trump had called commies could win such a haul, which pundits might call the mother of all blue tsunamis.

Comrades, the swamis would like a word about their accuracy ratings.

VIII
Consumer Confidence

No Refunds, No Returns

He broke ev'ry promise, of course, so mid-terms brought folks out in force. Since Donald's inflation and war gripped our nation, his voters felt buyer's remorse.

Store policy: exchanges accepted every two years.

IX
Rule of Law

The Pharaoh's Subpoena Season

For two years he ruled like a pharaoh, Corruption was free as a sparrow. But Donald's rejections in mid-term elections mean law-breaking options will narrow.

Coming soon to a committee room near you: oversight.

X
Department of Loyalty

All That Kneeling, For What?

For years we saw candidates suck up to Trump, the spectacular fuck-up. Though fealty was vast their fun couldn't last, since mid-term elections had snuck up.

Knee pads may be returned at the loading dock.

And… exhale. Please exhale responsibly. Gloating is permitted in moderation; the next election is only two years away.
