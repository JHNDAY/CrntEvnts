A Ballad for These Times

The Wreck of the MagaVerse

with apologies to Henry Wadsworth Longfellow
❦ ❦ ❦

It was the schooner MagaVerse,

That sailed the wintry sea;

And the skipper had taken the whole of the GOP

To bear him company.

Then up and spake an old Economist,

Had sailed through the Panic of 'Eight,

"I pray thee, put into yonder port,

Ere the tariffs seal our fate."

The skipper, he tapped a post on his phone,

And a scornful laugh laughed he;

He cut a rope from the Constitution,

And bound them to the mast with glee.

"O Skipper! I hear the courthouse bells,

Oh say, what may it be?"

"'Tis a witch-hunt bell on a rigged-up coast!"

And he steered for the open sea.

"O Skipper! I see a gleaming light,

Oh say, what may it be?"

But the skipper answered never a word;

He was posting endlessly.

And fast through the midnight dark and drear,

Through the whistling sleet and snow,

Like a sheeted ghost, the vessel swept

Tow'rds the reef called Told-You-So.

At daybreak, on the bleak sea-beach,

A fact-checker stood aghast,

To see what was left of the MAGA World,

Lashed close to a drifting mast.

Such was the wreck of the MagaVerse,

In the midnight and the snow!

Heaven save us all from a fate like this,

On the reef called Told-You-So!

⚓
After "The Wreck of the Hesperus" (1842) · Norman's Woe, Gloucester, Massachusetts
